What EXECUTE does

Quick Add

Type one sentence, or dictate it with the browser's speech recognition. EXECUTE shows what it understood before anything is saved.

Tasks with due dates

Track pending and completed work with priorities, due dates and repeating tasks that roll forward when you finish them.

Goals and breakdowns

Group tasks under an outcome, then turn a goal into a short list of suggested steps you can edit and accept.

Events and commitments

Keep scheduled time and promises made to other people separate from your task list.

Search and filters

Search across your tasks, goals, events and commitments, and filter the task list by status, priority or recurrence.

Reminders in the browser

Opt in to browser notifications so upcoming and overdue items on the dashboard do not slip past you.

How to start

Create an account or log in. Everything you add is private to your account: tasks, goals, events and commitments are only ever read back for the user who created them.

Once you are signed in, opening the home page takes you straight to your dashboard.
