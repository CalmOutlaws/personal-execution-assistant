How EXECUTE works

EXECUTE is a personal execution assistant. You write down what you intend to do, give it a due date and a priority, and then work through the list one action at a time.

Goals group related tasks under one outcome you are working towards. Commitments and progress analytics will be added in later milestones and will appear on this dashboard.
